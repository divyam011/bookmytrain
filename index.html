IRCTC Rules Overview

Essential guidelines for Aadhaar verification priorities and Tatkal timelines.

General Advance Reservation

  • General reserved booking windows open exactly 60 days before travel date.
  • Aadhaar-linked accounts gain opening priority from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM on Day 1.
  • Non-Aadhaar accounts can book starting afternoon shifts after priority queue clearings.
  • Verification is completed instantly online within the official IRCTC portal.

Tatkal Reservation Rules

  • Tatkal slots open exactly 1 day before journey date from source station.
  • AC quota tickets open at 10:00 AM IST; Non-AC quotas open at 11:00 AM IST.
  • OTP authentication is strictly required during checkout for all online bookings.
  • A maximum of 4 passengers are allowed per PNR for rapid checkouts.

Important Travel Advisories

Chart Timings: Booking charts are finalized 4 hours prior to train departure times. Early morning trains (departures between 5:00 AM and 2:00 PM) have their primary chart scheduled at 9:00 PM the prior evening.

Counter Bookings: Bookings processed offline through physical computerized PRS reservation counters are unaffected by Aadhaar rules and function normally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reserving train tickets

For general bookings, reserved train seats open exactly 60 days before the journey date (excluding travel date itself) starting at 8:00 AM IST.

To prioritize genuine travelers, IRCTC gives accounts with completed Aadhaar KYC authentication first priority on opening day. Non-authenticated users can book after these initial queues clear.

Tatkal booking opens at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes and 11:00 AM IST for non-AC classes, exactly one day prior to the train's departure. Authorized travel agents cannot purchase tickets in the first 30 minutes.

While optional for simple off-season routes, Aadhaar verification is highly recommended for festival rushes and is mandatory for booking online Tatkal quotas, helping curb ticket scalping.
