IRCTC Rules for 2026

A clear breakdown of the new Aadhaar-first booking changes and Tatkal requirements.

General Advance Reservation
  • • General reserved tickets open 60 days before journey date.
  • • Opening day priority window for Aadhaar-authenticated users.
  • • From Jan 12, 2026, Aadhaar users can book from 8:00 AM until midnight on opening day.
  • • Non-Aadhaar users can only book after the priority window closes.
Tatkal Booking Rules
  • • Tatkal opens one day before departure, excluding journey date.
  • • Aadhaar authentication is mandatory for online Tatkal booking.
  • • Aadhaar OTP is required for all Tatkal bookings, including counters and agents.
  • • AC Tatkal opens at 10:00 AM; Sleeper/Second Sitting opens at 11:00 AM.
  • • Agents cannot book in the first 30 minutes of Tatkal opening.
  • • Maximum four passengers per PNR; concessions are not allowed.
Additional Notes

• Reservation charts are generally prepared 8 hours before departure. Trains departing between 5 AM and 2 PM have the first chart prepared at 9 PM the previous night.

• Offline bookings at computerized PRS counters are not affected by the Aadhaar priority rules and continue under existing counter rules.
